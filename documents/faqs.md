## Refunds and Payments to IRS

### Q: How fast will I get my refund?

A: IRS refund timing:
- E-filed with direct deposit: 10-21 days
- E-filed with paper check: 3-4 weeks  
- Paper filed: 6-8 weeks

TaxPro files all returns electronically, so you'll get your refund as quickly as possible.

### Q: Can I get my refund faster?

A: The fastest way is to:
1. File electronically (which we always do)
2. Choose direct deposit
3. File early in the season (IRS processes faster before deadlines)

Beware of "refund advance" loans - they often come with high fees. It's better to wait for the actual IRS refund.

### Q: What if I owe taxes?

A: If you owe the IRS, you have options:
- Pay in full by the deadline (April 15)
- Set up an IRS payment plan (installment agreement)
- Request an offer in compromise (if you qualify)
- File an extension and pay what you can now

We can help you explore all options and set up payment arrangements with the IRS.

### Q: What if I can't afford to pay what I owe?

A: Don't panic! Options include:
- IRS payment plan: $31-225 setup fee, then monthly payments
- Partial payment plan: Pay what you can afford
- Currently not collectible status: If you truly can't pay
- Offer in compromise: Settle for less than you owe (if you qualify)

It's crucial to at least FILE your return on time, even if you can't pay. Failure to file penalties are much higher than failure to pay penalties.

## Audits and IRS Issues

### Q: What are my chances of being audited?

A: Audit rates are quite low:
- Income under $200,000: Less than 0.5% chance

Most audits are correspondence audits (by mail) rather than in-person meetings. Our Audit Support Guarantee covers guidance for all audit types.

### Q: What's the difference between LLC and S-Corp?

A: Great question! Here's a simple breakdown:

**LLC (Limited Liability Company):**
- Easy to set up and maintain
- Flexible management structure
- Taxed as sole proprietor by default (pass-through)
- Can elect S-Corp taxation if beneficial

**S-Corporation:**
- More formal structure with requirements
- Can save on self-employment taxes
- Must pay reasonable salary to owners
- More paperwork and compliance

We offer a Tax Planning Consultation ($249) where we can analyze which structure is best for YOUR specific situation.
