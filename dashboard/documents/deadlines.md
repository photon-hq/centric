## Penalties for Missing Deadlines

### Failure to File
- **5% per month** of unpaid taxes (maximum 25%)
- Minimum penalty of $485 or 100% of tax owed (whichever is less) if return is over 60 days late

### Failure to Pay
- **0.5% per month** of unpaid taxes (maximum 25%)
- Increases to 1% per month if a payment installment agreement is rejected

### Underpayment of Estimated Tax
- Penalty calculated quarterly based on federal short-term rate plus 3%
- Generally applies if you owe $1,000+ and didn't pay at least:
  - 90% of current year tax, OR
  - 100% of prior year tax (110% if AGI over $150,000)

### How to Avoid Penalties
1. File on time (even if you can't pay in full)
2. Request an extension by April 15
3. Make estimated tax payments throughout the year
4. Set up a payment plan with the IRS
5. Request penalty abatement (for first-time offenders or reasonable cause)

## Extension Information

### Automatic 6-Month Extension
Filing Form 4868 by April 15 gives you until October 15 to file. However:
- ✅ **Extends time to FILE**: Yes
- ❌ **Extends time to PAY**: No
- You must still pay estimated taxes by April 15
- Interest accrues on unpaid amounts
- Late payment penalties still apply

### Who Should File an Extension?
- Missing documents (W-2, 1099s, K-1s)
- Complex tax situations needing more time
- Major life events (divorce, inheritance, business sale)
- Simply want more time to review and optimize

### How to File an Extension
1. **Through TaxPro**: $49 (includes tax estimate and e-filing)
2. **IRS Free File**: Available on IRS.gov
3. **Automatic Extension**: Make an electronic payment by April 15 indicating it's for an extension

## Year-Round Tax Dates

### Monthly
- **15th of each month**: Payroll tax deposits (if semi-weekly depositor)
- **Last day of month**: Monthly payroll tax deposits (if monthly depositor)

### Quarterly
- **Last day of month after quarter**: Quarterly payroll tax return (Form 941)
- **State estimated tax payments**: Many states require quarterly payments

### Annually
- **January 31**: 
  - W-2 deadline (to employees and SSA)
  - 1099 deadline (to recipients)
  - Form 940 (Annual Federal Unemployment)
- **March 15**:
  - Partnership and S-Corp returns
- **May 15**:
  - Tax-exempt organizations (Form 990)
